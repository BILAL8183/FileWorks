1. Introduction

Welcome to Filediter (https://filediter.com/). Filediter is a web-based collection of utility tools designed to allow users to work with files, documents, and data directly through their web browser. Our available tools include URL Encoder, Word & Character Counter, Color Picker, Image Compressor, Image Editor, OCR Text Extractor, Document Enhancer, E-Sign PDF, PDF Merger, PDF Watermark, Image to PDF, PDF to Image, PDF Extractor, and related utilities.

Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website or use our tools, how that information is used, and your choices regarding your data. Please review this Privacy Policy to understand our data practices and your available choices.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We clearly distinguish between information you may provide voluntarily and standard technical information that may be collected automatically where applicable.

Voluntarily Provided Information

Information you choose to submit to us directly:

  • Contact or support information
  • Email address (if provided for inquiries)
  • Feedback, messages, or support requests
  • Other information you voluntarily submit

Automatically Collected Information

Standard technical logs and metadata processed when accessing the site:

  • IP address and approximate geographic region
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Referring URL and pages visited
  • Date/time of access and basic diagnostics

3. File and User Content Processing

Filediter provides a variety of client-side utility tools that process images, PDFs, documents, text, URLs, and other user-provided content.

Client-Side vs. Server-Side Processing:

Many Filediter tools are designed to process user content directly within the user's browser. Where a tool performs processing locally, the relevant file or content does not need to be uploaded to a Filediter server for that processing. If a particular feature relies on a third-party service, external API, or server-side functionality, the relevant data may be transmitted as necessary for that feature to operate.

We do not make unsupported claims regarding absolute zero data transmission where network requests are required for infrastructure, security, or feature delivery.

4. How We Use Information

We may use the information we collect for purposes including, but not limited to:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving the Filediter website and utilities.
  • Operating tools and features efficiently across various browsers and devices.
  • Fixing software bugs, optimizing performance, and troubleshooting technical issues.
  • Preventing abuse, fraud, and maintaining website security.
  • Responding to user support requests, questions, and feedback.
  • Understanding aggregate website usage and interaction trends where analytics are enabled.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and enforcing our Terms of Service.

Filediter does not sell your personal information as a business practice.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Cookies: Filediter or integrated third-party services may use cookies or similar tracking technologies where applicable to ensure essential functionality, remember user preferences, maintain security, and analyze website performance.

Browser Local Storage: The website may utilize browser localStorage or sessionStorage for convenience features such as saving favorites, remembering recently used tools, or storing user interface preferences (for example, your light or dark theme choice). LocalStorage data is stored directly in your web browser and can generally be cleared at any time through your browser settings. Local storage data is not encrypted unless specific application features implement client-side encryption.

6. Third-Party Services

To deliver a fast, secure, and reliable experience, Filediter may utilize third-party infrastructure and service providers necessary for website hosting (such as Vercel), font delivery (such as Google Fonts), icon libraries (Font Awesome), styling frameworks (Tailwind CSS CDN), content delivery networks for open-source code libraries (such as cdnjs), analytics, and security. When your browser loads resources from these providers, they may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details.

These third-party services operate under their own respective privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies for information regarding their data practices.

7. Analytics

Filediter may use analytics technologies to understand how visitors interact with the website. If analytics are enabled, these technologies may collect information such as pages visited, approximate usage patterns, device and browser information, and other technical telemetry to help us improve user experience. You may opt out of analytics tracking where browser settings or extensions permit.

8. Data Security

We employ reasonable technical, organizational, and administrative security measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include HTTPS encryption where supported, regular maintenance, and secure hosting environments.

Disclaimer: Although we take reasonable measures to protect information, no method of transmission or electronic storage over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, provide our services, maintain internal records, resolve disputes, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

For browser-local data (such as local storage), information remains in your browser until manually cleared by you or removed by the application.

10. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share or disclose your personal information except where reasonably necessary, including:

  • With trusted service providers, hosting partners, and security vendors assisting in website operation.
  • With analytics providers where enabled to evaluate site performance.
  • To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or enforceable governmental requests.
  • To protect the rights, property, safety, and security of Filediter, our users, or the public.
  • In connection with any potential corporate merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, where applicable.

11. International Data Transfers

Because Filediter utilizes cloud hosting and global third-party infrastructure providers, information collected through the website may be processed, stored, and transferred in countries other than your country of residence, where data protection laws may differ. By using Filediter, you acknowledge and consent to such potential international transfers where applicable.

12. Children's Privacy

Filediter is a general audience utility website and is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or higher threshold where required by local law). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

13. User Rights and Choices

Depending on your geographic location and applicable local data protection laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, which may include:

  • Access: Requesting confirmation of whether we process your data and obtaining a copy.
  • Correction: Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Requesting deletion of your personal information where applicable.
  • Restriction and Objection: Requesting restriction of or objecting to certain data processing activities.
  • Data Portability: Receiving your data in a structured format where applicable.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights where applicable, please reach out to us using the contact details provided below.

14. California Privacy Rights

If California privacy laws (such as the CCPA/CPRA) apply to Filediter and to you as a California resident, you may have specific rights regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal information. Filediter does not sell personal information for monetary consideration. For inquiries regarding California privacy rights, please contact us.

15. European Economic Area (EEA) and UK Privacy Rights

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR applies to our processing of your personal information, you benefit from specific data protection rights (such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability). Filediter processes such information based on legitimate operational interests, user consent, or contractual necessity as applicable.

16. Do Not Track Signals

Some web browsers feature "Do Not Track" (DNT) settings that signal to websites that you do not want to have your online activity tracked. Because there is no universally accepted standard for interpreting DNT signals, Filediter may not currently respond to browser DNT signals.

17. External Links

Filediter may contain links to external third-party websites or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of such external websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, legal requirements, or services. When we update the policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Changes become effective immediately upon posting. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed of our privacy practices.
