How to Combine PDF Files Online Safely

Combining reports, invoices, scanned pages, or e-books into a unified single document has never been easier. Our **PDF Merger Tool** allows you to merge multiple PDF files right inside your web browser without risking sensitive personal information.

Most conventional online PDF joiners upload your files to remote web servers, creating data privacy and compliance risks. FileWorks uses modern WebAssembly technology (`pdf-lib`) to read, sequence, and stitch PDF documents in local memory.

Key Features of Our PDF Binder

  • Flexible File Ordering: Move PDF items up or down to ensure pages follow your preferred chronological order.
  • Page Count Preview: View individual PDF page counts prior to compilation.
  • Lossless Merge Quality: Retains high-resolution vectors, embedded fonts, images, and text formatting.
