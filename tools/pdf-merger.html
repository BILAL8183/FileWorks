    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?

    There is no arbitrary file count cap. Because merging runs locally using your device's memory and CPU, you can combine as many PDF files as your browser memory comfortably supports (up to 150 MB total recommended).

    Are my confidential documents uploaded to a server?

    No. Your PDF files are read, reordered, and merged directly inside your browser using pdf-lib. Your documents never leave your device.

    Does merging PDFs reduce image or text quality?

    No. The merger copies the original PDF page streams losslessly into the new document, preserving all vector text, embedded fonts, high-resolution images, and page dimensions.

    Can I merge password-protected PDF files?

    Encrypted or password-locked PDFs cannot be merged until they are unlocked. If you select a locked PDF, the tool will alert you so you can unlock it first.
