Why Use an Online Word & Character Counter?

Whether you are an SEO content strategist, copywriter, software developer, or academic researcher, staying within word and character thresholds is essential. Filediter's online word counter and SEO text analyzer processes your writing directly inside your browser—evaluating word frequency, 2-word and 3-word long-tail phrases, Flesch readability scores, and SERP snippet limits in real time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text stored on any server?

No. All text counting, readability scoring, and keyword extraction happen locally inside your web browser. If you leave "Auto-save in browser" enabled, your draft is saved only in your own device's localStorage.

How are reading time, speaking time, and readability calculated?

Reading time is computed using an average adult silent reading speed of 200 words per minute (WPM), and speaking time at 130 WPM. Readability uses the standard Flesch Reading Ease formula comparing average words per sentence and average syllables per word.

What is a good SEO keyword density percentage?

A natural primary keyword density typically falls between 1% and 2.5%. Checking the 2-Word and 3-Word tabs helps ensure your target topic phrases appear naturally without triggering search engine over-optimization filters.
