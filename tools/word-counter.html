Why Use an Online Word & Character Counter?

Whether you are a blog writer, SEO specialist, copywriter, or student, tracking word limits and character limits is critical. Our online word counter provides live calculations for your text directly inside your web browser. From Google search engine meta tag optimization to academic paper submissions, having accurate statistics ensures your writing complies with strict guidelines.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is my text stored on any server?

No. All text processing is executed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never stored or sent anywhere.

How is reading and speaking time calculated?

Reading time is computed based on an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute (WPM), and speaking time at 130 WPM.
