Why Use an Online Word & Character Counter?

Whether you are a blog writer, SEO specialist, copywriter, or student, tracking word limits and character limits is critical. Our online word counter provides live calculations for your text directly inside your web browser. From Google search engine meta tag optimization to academic paper submissions, having accurate statistics ensures your writing complies with strict guidelines.

Key Features

  • Instant word and character counts without page reloads.
  • Calculates spaces, sentences, and total paragraphs.
  • Estimates reading time based on standard 200 WPM reading speeds.
  • Extracts top keywords and calculates SEO density percentages.
  • Quick text case converter (UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case).

Common Limits Supported

  • Google Meta Title: ~60 characters
  • Google Meta Description: ~160 characters
  • X / Twitter Post: 280 characters
  • Instagram Caption: 2,200 characters
  • LinkedIn Post: 3,000 characters

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is my text stored on any server?

No. All text processing is executed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never stored or sent anywhere.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is computed based on an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute (WPM).
