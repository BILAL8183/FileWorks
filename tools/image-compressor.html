How to Compress and Resize Images Step-by-Step

  1. Upload Image: Click the dropzone or drag and drop your JPG, PNG, or WEBP file.
  2. Set Dimensions: Input custom Width and Height either in Pixels or Percent. Use the lock icon to maintain aspect ratio.
  3. Choose Format & Quality: Pick your preferred output format and set compression quality level.
  4. Download: Click 'Resize Image' and save your optimized photo instantly.
