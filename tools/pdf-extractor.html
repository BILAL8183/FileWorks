How to Extract or Remove Specific Pages from a PDF

Whether you need to pull a single signed page out of a 50-page contract, split a scanned book into separate chapters, or delete blank pages before sending an attachment, Filediter's online PDF page extractor gives you full visual control without losing vector text quality.

  1. Upload your PDF: Click the upload box or drag and drop your PDF file into the workspace.
  2. Select your pages: Click individual page thumbnails, hold Shift to select a range, or type page ranges such as 1-3, 5, 9-12.
  3. Download the result: Choose whether to merge the selected pages into one PDF, split them into individual PDFs inside a ZIP archive, or delete the selected pages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDF is opened, previewed, and split 100% locally in your browser using open-source JavaScript libraries. Your sensitive files never leave your device.

Can I save each selected page as a separate PDF file?

Yes! In the Output Action Mode dropdown, choose "Split Selected into Separate PDFs (.ZIP)". Each selected page will be saved as its own standalone PDF inside a single convenient ZIP download.

Does extracting pages preserve original quality and selectable text?

Yes. Pages are copied directly at the native PDF object level without re-encoding images or flattening text, so 100% of your original quality, fonts, and searchable text remain intact.
