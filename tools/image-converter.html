    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can I convert a transparent PNG or WebP to JPG?

    Yes. Because JPEG does not support transparent pixels, any transparent areas are automatically filled with the color selected in the Background Color picker (white #ffffff by default).

    Can I convert WebP to PNG or JPG?

    Yes. Add your WebP images, choose PNG (for lossless quality and transparency) or JPG (for universal compatibility with older apps), and click Convert All Images.

    Why is my converted PNG larger than the original JPG?

    PNG is a lossless format designed for graphics, logos, and screenshots with sharp edges. Photographs contain millions of subtle color gradients that compress much smaller in lossy formats like WebP or JPG.

    Is batch image conversion completely private?

    Yes. All image decoding, resizing, format encoding, and ZIP archiving happen locally in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any external server.
