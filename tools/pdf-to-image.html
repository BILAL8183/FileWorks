Choosing the right image format and resolution

JPG (.jpg) for everyday sharing

Ideal for photographs, brochures, and general documents where compact file size and universal compatibility across all apps matter most.

PNG (.png) for sharp typography

Uses lossless compression that keeps fine text, line art, charts, and technical diagrams free from compression artifacts.

WEBP (.webp) for modern websites

Delivers superior compression for web publishing and CMS uploads while maintaining high visual fidelity.

Frequently asked questions

Which image format should I choose: JPG, PNG, or WEBP?

Choose JPG (.jpg) for smaller file sizes and everyday sharing, PNG (.png) for lossless sharpness on text-heavy pages or diagrams, and WEBP (.webp) for modern web publishing with high compression.

What resolution (DPI) should I use when converting a PDF to images?

2x High Quality (~150–200 DPI) is ideal for most screens and documents. Choose 3x Ultra-HD (~300 DPI) when you need crisp print-ready images or fine small-print legibility.

Are my PDF pages uploaded to a server during conversion?

No. Every PDF page is rendered onto an HTML5 canvas directly inside your browser using PDF.js and packaged into a ZIP archive locally with JSZip. Your files never leave your device.

Can I download individual pages instead of the full ZIP file?

Yes. Each rendered page card includes a dedicated Save button so you can download specific pages one by one, or click Download All Pages (ZIP) to save the entire document at once.
