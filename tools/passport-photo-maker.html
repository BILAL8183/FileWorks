How to Take & Prepare an Accepted Passport Photo

Simple tips for creating compliant biometric ID photos from home.

Biometric Head Alignment

Position the top of your head (crown) and chin inside the dashed oval markers, keep your eyes level with the eye line, and maintain a neutral expression looking straight at the camera.

True 300 DPI Physical Sizing

Exported PNG files include embedded 300 DPI physical resolution metadata (pHYs chunk), ensuring exact millimeter dimensions when printed at 100% scale.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool guarantee visa or passport acceptance?

This tool produces exact physical and pixel dimensions at 300 DPI and provides a biometric head alignment guide. However, passport agencies also evaluate lighting, shadows, eyeglasses, facial expression, and background uniformity—always check your destination country's official guidelines.

Which photo sizes are available?

Built-in presets include 35×45 mm, 2×2 inches (50.8×50.8 mm), 35×50 mm, 33×48 mm, 40×60 mm, and 30×40 mm. You can also choose "Custom Dimensions (mm)" to enter any exact width and height required by your form.

How do I print multiple passport photos on 4×6 inch paper?

Click the "Print Sheet" preview tab or the "Download 4×6 Print Sheet" button. The tool automatically arranges as many copies as fit onto a 4×6 inch (or 5×7 / A4) canvas at 300 DPI with proper spacing and scissor cut lines.

Does it automatically remove backgrounds?

No—take your portrait in front of a plain white or off-white wall with even lighting. If you upload a transparent PNG or WebP image that already has its background removed, you can use the Backdrop Fill Color swatches to apply an official white, light grey, or light blue background.
