How to Reduce PDF File Size Without Losing Readability

Large PDF files often fail to send over email or get rejected by university, visa, and job application portals with strict 2 MB or 5 MB file size limits. Most bloated PDFs contain uncompressed high-DPI camera scans or embedded metadata.

With Filediter's online PDF compressor, you can downscale page resolution to a crisp ~115 DPI (Balanced) or ~83 DPI (Extreme), convert color scans to Grayscale for an extra 30–50% reduction, or run a Lossless rewrite when you need selectable text preserved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this make every PDF smaller?

Scanned PDFs and image-heavy files typically shrink by 40% to 85%. If a PDF is already a small, pure-text vector document, rasterizing its pages into images can sometimes make it larger—if that happens, the tool alerts you automatically and lets you switch to Lossless mode or keep the smaller original.

Will the compressed PDF still have selectable text?

The Lossless preset preserves all selectable text, clickable links, forms, and vector paths. The Balanced, Extreme, and High Quality presets flatten each page into an optimized JPEG image to achieve major file size reductions.

Can I compress scanned PDFs?

Yes. Image-based compression is specifically designed for scanned PDFs. Enabling the Convert pages to Grayscale checkbox reduces scanned document sizes even further while keeping text easy to read.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. This tool processes your document entirely inside your browser using open-source WebAssembly/JavaScript libraries loaded from public CDNs. Your file contents are never sent to Filediter or any third-party server.
