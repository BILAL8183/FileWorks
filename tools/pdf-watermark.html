Stamp PDF Documents Safely Online

Watermarking a PDF labels draft versions, marks contracts as confidential, and discourages unauthorized redistribution. This PDF watermark tool lets you add text stamps such as "CONFIDENTIAL" or "DRAFT", or your own transparent company logo, directly in your web browser.

The tool runs on the open-source pdf-lib and PDF.js libraries inside your browser, so your document stays on your device while you work and is never sent to external servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is opened and edited in your browser, and the watermarked file is created on your device. The page itself loads open-source libraries from public CDNs, but your document is never sent anywhere.

Can I use my own logo as a watermark?

Yes. Open the Image Logo tab and upload a PNG, JPG, or WebP file. Transparent PNG and WebP backgrounds are preserved, and you can set the size, opacity, angle, and position.

Does the text watermark support Arabic, Urdu, or Chinese?

Text watermarks use the standard PDF fonts, which cover Latin characters only. For other scripts, create an image of your text and add it with the Image Logo tab.

Can someone remove the watermark?

The watermark is added as regular page content. It works as a visible label, not as tamper-proof protection, and someone with PDF editing software may be able to remove it.
