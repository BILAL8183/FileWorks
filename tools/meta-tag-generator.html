What Are Meta Tags and Why Are They Important for SEO?

Meta tags are snippets of text and HTML tags placed inside the <head> section of a webpage. They provide search engine bots (like Googlebot) and social media platforms (like Facebook, X/Twitter, and LinkedIn) critical structured metadata regarding your site's content, title, preview graphics, and indexing directives.

Essential Tags Created

  • Title Tag: Primary click title for search engine results pages.
  • Meta Description: Summary snippet shown under title in Google.
  • Canonical Tag: Prevents duplicate content index issues.
  • Open Graph Tags: Controls title, desc & banner image on Facebook & LinkedIn.
  • Twitter Cards: Renders large media preview cards on X/Twitter.

SEO Best Practices

  • Keep Meta Titles under 60 characters to prevent truncation.
  • Keep Meta Descriptions under 160 characters.
  • Use Open Graph images with a recommended aspect ratio of 1200 x 630 pixels.
  • Always specify a canonical URL to consolidate page authority.
