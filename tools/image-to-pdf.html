Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded to any server?

No. All image decoding, resizing, and PDF compilation happen locally inside your web browser. Your personal documents never leave your device.

How do I reorder or rotate images before creating the PDF?

Drag any thumbnail card in the grid to move it to a new page position, or click the Rotate icon on any card to rotate that specific image by 90 degrees.

What is the difference between A4 and "Fit Page to Each Image Size"?

A4 and US Letter give every page in your PDF uniform standard paper dimensions—ideal for printing. Fit Page to Each Image Size sets each PDF page's exact dimensions to match the photo itself with no extra whitespace.
