CamScanner Quality Document Enhancement Online

Transform shadowy, yellowed, or dim photos of receipts, notes, and legal paperwork into crystal-clear digital documents. Our **Document Enhancer & CamScanner Filter** uses client-side GPU canvas rendering to sharpen fonts, remove background noise, and sharpen contrast.

Unlike traditional online scanning services that force you to upload private personal documents to remote cloud storage, FileWorks renders everything locally inside your browser canvas.

Enhancement Presets Explained

  • Magic Color: Increases contrast and color vibrancy while brightening yellow paper backgrounds.
  • B&W Scan: Converts the document into high-contrast black and white text for crisp printing.
  • Grayscale: Removes color tint while preserving original photographic tones.
  • Sharp Text: Boosts midtone contrast to make faint handwriting and small font prints readable.
