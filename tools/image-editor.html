Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded to any server?

No. Every edit—cropping, resizing, color grading, drawing, and watermarking—runs locally on your device inside your browser.

How do I undo a crop, brush stroke, or text stamp?

Click the Undo button in the top bar above the image (or press Ctrl+Z on your keyboard). You can also click Revert Original to start fresh from your uploaded photo.

Can I paste an image directly from my clipboard?

Yes! Take a screenshot or copy any image and press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on Mac) anywhere on the page to load it into the editor immediately.
